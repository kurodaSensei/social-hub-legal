Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-05

social-hub is a personal tool built by Alfredo Romero (@kurodacafe) to view his own metrics from Instagram, TikTok and YouTube in one place. It runs as a local macOS app and is not distributed to the public.

What data is collected

The app reads only the owner's own content and metrics from the platform APIs he has explicitly authorized (Instagram Graph API, TikTok Display API, YouTube Data API). No data from other users is collected.

Where data is stored

All data is stored locally in a SQLite database on the owner's own computer. OAuth tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain. No data is sent to any third-party server. No analytics, telemetry or crash reports are collected.

Third parties

The app does not share data with any third party. The only outbound connections are to the official APIs of Instagram (Meta), TikTok and YouTube (Google), strictly to refresh the owner's own metrics.

Access and deletion

Because the app is for personal use, there is no other user to request access or deletion. Disconnecting a network deletes its tokens from the Keychain and stops any further sync.
